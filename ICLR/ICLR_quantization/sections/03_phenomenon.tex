\section{A silent, protocol-dependent failure}
\label{sec:phenomenon}
\paragraph{Setup.}
The main experiments use Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct \citep{grattafiori2024llama3}, Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct \citep{qwen2025qwen25} and Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 \citep{jiang2023mistral}; the census of \cref{sec:universality} covers \nCensus{} instruction-tuned models from 0.5B to 32B parameters.
Weights are quantised to 3 or 4 bits (symmetric, group size 128, act-order, $\rho=0.05$) and kept on the integer grid in 16-bit storage so that every arm shares the same inference code.
The \emph{short-document} protocol tokenises 128 C4 documents \citep{raffel2020c4} separately (mean 420 tokens), as GPTQModel \citep{gptqmodel} and LLM Compressor \citep{llmcompressor} do by default; the \emph{window} protocol draws 128 random windows of 2,048 tokens, as in the original GPTQ code, from C4 (c4win) or the Pile \citep{gao2021pile}.
IFEval \citep{zhou2023ifeval} is reported as the mean of prompt- and instruction-level strict and loose accuracy over 541 prompts with greedy decoding; a different calibration seed moves it by up to \SeedNoise{} points, which we treat as noise.
Multi-IF \citep{he2024multiif}, GSM8K \citep{cobbe2021gsm8k}, MMLU \citep{hendrycks2021mmlu} and WikiText-2 perplexity \citep{merity2017wikitext} are described in \cref{app:protocols}.

\paragraph{Collapse that likelihood does not show.}
Under the short-document protocol, 3-bit GPTQ leaves Llama at \LgptqIF{} and Qwen at \QgptqIF{} on IFEval, 30 to 40 points below RTN at the same bit-width (\cref{fig:teaser}, \cref{tab:collapse}): a step whose only purpose is to reduce each layer's reconstruction error has made the model worse than no compensation.
The collapsed Llama model answers most prompts with unformatted or truncated text, so GSM8K falls to \LgptqGSM{} (RTN \LrtnGSM{}) and the loss persists over every turn of Multi-IF.
The same checkpoints have an MMLU of \LgptqMMLU{} and \QgptqMMLU{} (fp16 \LfpMMLU{} and \QfpMMLU{}) and ordinary 3-bit perplexities.
Both metrics are teacher-forced: they score the likelihood of given text and never visit the states the model produces when writing its own response, whereas the damage, as \cref{sec:lesion} shows, sits on the prompt-template tokens that steer generation.

\paragraph{Dependence on how the calibration text is cut.}
With 2,048-token windows in place of short documents, Llama recovers to \LcwinIF{} and Qwen to \QcwinIF{}; chat-formatted text, whether the same documents wrapped in the chat template (\LcchatIF{} / \QcchatIF{}) or UltraChat conversations \citep{ding2023ultrachat} (\LuchatIF{} / \QuchatIF{}), also removes the collapse (\cref{tab:protocols_result}).
The effect is not monotone in sample length: Llama collapses again with 32 WikiText windows, Qwen with Pile windows, and Mistral-7B-v0.3 collapses under c4win (\MgptqIF{}, RTN \MrtnIF{}) but not under short documents (\MshortIF{}).
The two protocols that disagree are both defaults.
We treat the dependence as a symptom; \cref{sec:lesion} locates it in a specific part of the quantised network.

\paragraph{Which quantisers share it.}
Sorting six quantisers by their objective (\cref{tab:methods}) draws one line.
Methods that compensate rounding error through the input-side curvature of \cref{eq:layerwise} collapse: GPTQ on both models, GPTAQ on Llama; rotating the model first, as QuaRot does, removes the collapse (\LrotIF{} / \QrotIF{}) by spreading the sink token's activation over all channels, which is the condition \cref{sec:mechanism} identifies.
AutoRound, which learns rounding offsets by a token-averaged block loss and has no OBS step, collapses on both models; RTN, HQQ \citep{badri2023hqq} and AWQ, which fit no reconstruction objective or only per-channel scales, do not.
The dividing line is whether the method minimises a token-averaged reconstruction objective with freedom to move error between channels or tokens; the rest of the paper follows the OBS family, where the damage can be derived in closed form.
