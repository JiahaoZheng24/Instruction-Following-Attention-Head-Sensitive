\section{Generality: models and quantizers}
\label{sec:universality}
Every statement below was a written prediction before the models were run (\cref{tab:census,tab:census_full}).
The premise, \cref{prop:mismatch}, was measured on 28 instruction-tuned models, \nCensus{} of which were also quantized; it holds in every one of the \nSink{} with a sink-dominated down-projection ($A_{\bos}\ge\AbosMin$, $G_{\bos}\le\GbosMax$; \cref{fig:ag} in \cref{app:extra}) and in neither gemma-2 model, whose architecture has no such layer (largest $A_{\bos}$ \GemmaAbosMax{}).
The lesion follows wherever the premise holds and the quantizer is OBS-based: in each of the \nSinkStats{} census models with a sink, GPTQ's error on the template positions exceeds RTN's by \LesionRatioMin{} to \LesionRatioMax{} times, in \nRowConc{} the largest row carries more than twice its RTN share, and in \nClipOver{} the largest compensated entry exceeds twice the largest original one (\cref{cor:shape}(i), (iii)); the signature is present under GPTQModel's group-aware column order as well, confined to one template position and a hundred times smaller in mass (\cref{app:protocols}).
Collapse does not follow: \nCollapse{} of \nCensus{} models collapse under short documents and one of \nWin{} under c4win, models with lesions as large as Nemo's or Qwen2.5-32B's do not, and the lesion's size neither sets a threshold nor ranks the losses (Spearman $-0.35$); collapse needs the lesion and the failure of its neighbors to absorb it (\cref{sec:lesion}).
In this census the corrected objective never falls more than \CorrWorstVsRTN{} points below RTN on \nCorr{} models, the one case beyond noise being gemma-2-9b, which has no sink and nothing to repair; it repairs every 3-bit collapse we found and two of the three 4-bit losses, and on the \nHealthy{} non-collapsing models it changes IFEval by \CorrHealthyMean{} on average (\CorrHealthyWorst{} to \CorrHealthyBest{}).

\paragraph{Across quantizers.}
\cref{prop:mismatch} is a property of the objective, so it sorts quantizers by their objective rather than their name (\cref{tab:methods}): methods that compensate through the input-side curvature collapse, GPTQ on both models and GPTAQ on Llama, though not GPTQ under GPTQModel's group-aware column order (\cref{tab:methods}); rotating first (QuaRot) removes the collapse (\LrotIF{} / \QrotIF{}); AutoRound, a token-averaged block loss with free rounding offsets and no OBS step, collapses Qwen (\QarIF{}) and loses ten points to RTN on Llama (\LarIF{}); RTN, HQQ \citep{badri2023hqq} and AWQ, which fit no reconstruction objective or only per-channel scales, lose nothing.
The dividing line is a token-averaged reconstruction objective with freedom to move error between weights; \cref{thm:rankone} derives the damage for the OBS family, AutoRound's is consistent with \cref{prop:mismatch} but not derived here.
