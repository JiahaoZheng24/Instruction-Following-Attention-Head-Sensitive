\section{Protocols and implementation details}
\label{app:protocols}

\subsection{Calibration sampling in the reference implementations}
We verified the sampling of each protocol against the source of the implementation that uses it (September 2026).
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{GPTQ reference code} \citep{frantar2023gptq}: draws documents of at least 2,048 tokens and cuts one random 2,048-token window from each; our c4win and Pile protocols follow this.
\item \textbf{GPTQModel} \citep{gptqmodel}, \texttt{utils/calibration.py}: tokenises each calibration row separately, does not truncate below the model's maximum length and does not concatenate rows by default; the README example takes 1,024 rows of C4. Its \texttt{QuantizeConfig} defaults are symmetric quantisation, group size 128, \texttt{damp\_percent} 0.05, \texttt{true\_sequential} on and \texttt{desc\_act} unset. Our short-document protocol reproduces its sampling with 128 rows; with 1,024 rows and the same damping Llama gives \LgptqLibIF{} and Qwen \QgptqLibIF{}.
\item \textbf{LLM Compressor} \citep{llmcompressor}, \texttt{transformers/data/base.py}: truncates each sample to \texttt{max\_seq\_length} and does not concatenate by default, the same sampling shape; its documented example for instruction-tuned models uses chat-formatted data, which is one of the remedies of \cref{cor:shape}.
\end{itemize}
Our quantiser matches the reference loop of GPTQ with act-order and $\rho=0.05$ and collects the Hessians of the seven matrices of a block from the block's unquantised input (no true-sequential pass); RTN uses the same grouping and symmetric grid.

\subsection{A correction to our layer-wise loop}
An earlier version of our loop passed the keyword arguments captured from the model's forward call, including an automatically created key-value cache, into the per-layer calls.
Because each layer is called twice per sample (once to accumulate the Hessian, once to propagate after quantisation), the second call attended the layer's pre-quantisation keys and values.
Every number in this paper comes from the corrected loop, which we validated by comparing the propagated activations at every depth with a full forward pass of the quantised model (relative error $0$ at all layers for Llama and Qwen).
On the seven headline arms the correction moved IFEval by at most 1.5 points on six and by 22 points on one marginal arm (Llama under Pile windows, from a collapsed to a healthy value); the direction of every conclusion was unchanged.
