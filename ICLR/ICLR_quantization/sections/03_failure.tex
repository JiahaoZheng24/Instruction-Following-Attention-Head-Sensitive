\section{A silent, protocol-dependent failure}
\label{sec:phenomenon}
\paragraph{Setup.}
The main experiments use Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct \citep{grattafiori2024llama3}, Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct \citep{qwen2025qwen25} and Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 \citep{jiang2023mistral}; the census of \cref{sec:universality} covers \nCensus{} instruction-tuned models from 1.7B to 32B.
Weights are quantized to 3 or 4 bits (symmetric, group size 128, act-order, $\rho=0.05$) and kept on the integer grid in 16-bit storage; \cref{tab:defaults} separates library defaults from our choices.
GPTQModel's own default loop, which orders the columns group by group, collapses neither model, whereas act-order or the natural order inside it collapses Llama (\cref{tab:library}): the failure is that of the reference loop under the libraries' sampling.
The \emph{short-document} protocol tokenizes 128 C4 documents \citep{raffel2020c4} separately (mean 420 tokens), as GPTQModel \citep{gptqmodel} and LLM Compressor \citep{llmcompressor} do by default; the \emph{window} protocol draws 128 random 2,048-token windows, as the original GPTQ code does, from C4 (c4win) or the Pile \citep{gao2021pile}.
IFEval \citep{zhou2023ifeval} is the mean of prompt- and instruction-level strict and loose accuracy over 541 prompts, greedy decoding; a different calibration draw moves a non-collapsing arm by up to \SeedNoise{} points, which we treat as noise; Multi-IF \citep{he2024multiif}, GSM8K \citep{cobbe2021gsm8k}, MMLU \citep{hendrycks2021mmlu} and WikiText-2 perplexity \citep{merity2017wikitext} are described in \cref{app:protocols}.
On Llama and Mistral the chat template and the tokenizer each write a \bos{}, so every generation prompt carries two; a single-\bos{} control moves no conclusion (\cref{app:protocols}).

\paragraph{Collapse that likelihood does not show.}
Round-to-nearest, the reference throughout, is GPTQ without the compensation step, so any gap between the two is the compensation's own doing.
Under the short-document protocol, 3-bit GPTQ leaves Llama at \LgptqIF{} and Qwen at \QgptqIF{}, 41 and 27 points below RTN (\cref{fig:bars}, \cref{tab:collapse}): a step whose only purpose is to reduce each layer's reconstruction error has made the model worse than no compensation.
The collapsed Llama answers most prompts with unformatted or truncated text, so GSM8K falls to \LgptqGSM{} (RTN \LrtnGSM{}) and the loss persists over every turn of Multi-IF, yet the same checkpoints have an MMLU above their RTN counterparts (\LgptqMMLU{} and \QgptqMMLU{} against \LrtnMMLU{} and \QrtnMMLU{}) and Qwen's perplexity is better than RTN's (\QgptqPPL{} against \QrtnPPL{}); Llama's perplexity (\LgptqPPL{} against \LrtnPPL{}) is the one likelihood signal that moves, far less than the generation.
The metrics miss the collapse because the damage sits on the chat-template tokens that steer generation (\cref{sec:lesion}), which neither WikiText nor a few-shot MMLU prompt contains.

\paragraph{Dependence on how the calibration text is cut.}
With 2,048-token windows in place of short documents Llama recovers to \LcwinIF{} and Qwen to \QcwinIF{}, and chat-formatted text, the same documents in the chat template or UltraChat \citep{ding2023ultrachat}, also removes the collapse (\cref{fig:bars}b, \cref{tab:protocols_result}).
It is not sample length alone: Qwen collapses again under Pile and WikiText windows, and Mistral-7B-v0.3 collapses under C4 windows (\MgptqIF{}, RTN \MrtnIF{}) but not under short documents; nor the corpus: 128 Pile documents tokenized separately collapse Llama and Qwen as C4 does; nor the token count: 1,024 short documents, more tokens than 128 windows, leave Llama at \LgptqLibIF{} (\cref{tab:defaults}).
Its effect on Llama is visible in the calibration Hessians before any weight is rounded: the \bos{} token holds \AbosZero{} of the trace at the layer-0 down-projection under short documents against \AbosZeroWin{} under C4 windows, \AbosZeroPile{} under Pile windows and \AbosZeroWiki{} under WikiText, so a second matrix becomes \bos{}-dominated exactly under the protocol that collapses (\cref{sec:lesion}).
Both protocols are library defaults, and the collapse is reproducible across draws with one exception: Llama collapses in \LcollapseFreq{} draws (\LseedRange{}; the fifth gives \LgptqSeedIF{}), Qwen in \QcollapseFreq{}, Mistral under C4 windows in \McollapseFreq{} (\cref{tab:seeds}).
The exception says that collapse is a property of the sample and the model together (\cref{sec:lesion}).
