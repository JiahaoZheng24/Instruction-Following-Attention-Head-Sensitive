\subsection*{Ethics statement}
This work studies a failure mode of a widely used compression method and proposes a repair.
Its most direct consequence is that quantised instruction-tuned models validated only by perplexity may be deployed in a state in which they no longer follow instructions; we consider disclosure of the failure and of its cause to be in the interest of users.
No human subjects or private data were involved.
