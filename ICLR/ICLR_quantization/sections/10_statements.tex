\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
All quantization, evaluation and analysis code, the run index that maps every number in this paper to a checkpoint and a calibration protocol, and the job scripts whose headers state each batch's prediction before it ran are released at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/sink-hessian-ptq-D7BD}.
\cref{app:protocols} gives the calibration sampling of each protocol as verified against the library sources, the quantizer settings, the evaluation settings and the seeds; \cref{app:proofs} gives the assumptions and proofs behind \cref{sec:mechanism}.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}
This work studies a failure mode of a widely used compression method and proposes a repair.
Its most direct consequence is that quantized instruction-tuned models validated only by perplexity may be deployed in a state in which they no longer follow instructions; we consider disclosure of the failure and of its cause to be in the interest of users.
No human subjects or private data were involved.

\subsection*{AI use statement}
The authors designed the study, wrote the pre-registered predictions, derived the theory and its proofs, ran and checked every experiment and drew the conclusions.
Large language model assistants were used under the authors' direction for drafting and refactoring code, for literature search and for editing the text and figures; every line of code was run and checked, every number traced to a run in the released index and every reference verified against its source by the authors, who take full responsibility for the content.
